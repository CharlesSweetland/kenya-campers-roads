# Privacy and consent

The map exists to share what roads are like, not who drove them. These rules apply to every report.

## What we publish

- The road, the section, the date it was driven, the condition, the type of vehicle, the weather, a short description, and any approved photo.
- Reports are credited to "Kenya Campers community", never to a person.

## What we never publish

- Names, usernames, phone numbers or email addresses.
- Who submitted a report. Form submissions don't collect email addresses. If you upload a photo Google asks you to sign in, but your name is removed from the file before it is shared.
- Photos showing identifiable faces or number plates.
- Live locations. A report says a road was driven on a date, never where someone is now or where they're camping tonight.

## Where reports come from

1. **The maintainer's own notes.** That is all the prototype contains.
2. **The Google Form.** Each submitter ticks a box giving permission to publish their update anonymously. A moderator reviews every one before it appears.
3. **Past WhatsApp messages in the Kenya Campers group.** These are used only with the community's permission, given through the group admins. They are rewritten as short summaries, not quoted word for word, with every personal detail removed. Members can ask for any summary based on their messages to be removed.

## Photos

Before it's published, every photo is checked for faces, number plates and anything that shows exactly where someone lives or is staying. Phones can embed GPS location in photos. Photos in this repository have that data stripped. Photos uploaded through the form are shown through Google's image preview, which doesn't include the original file's location data.

## Removing a report

Open an issue in this repository or message a group admin, saying which road and date. We'll take it down, without needing a reason.

## Who runs this

The map is maintained by volunteers from the Kenya Campers community. It isn't a commercial service and doesn't use analytics or cookies. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, whose servers see standard web request data. See the [OSM privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
